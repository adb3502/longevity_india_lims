BHARAT Study

Biomarkers of Healthy Aging, Resilience, Adversity, and Transitions - A comprehensive longitudinal study investigating aging biomarkers in the Indian population.

Study Features

• Multi-omics analysis (genomics, proteomics, metabolomics)
• Longitudinal design with 4 visits over 24 months
• Age cohorts from 20-70 years
• Indian population-specific demographics
